# King River Cafe - Research Findings

## Name
The establishment is most commonly known as **King River Cafe** (without the accent on 'e').

## Contact Information
- **Email:** on@netc.net.au (Source: Northeast Directory, Milawa Gourmet)
- **Phone:** (03) 5727 3461
- **Official Website:** There is no active official website. Previously, www.kingrivercafe.com.au was used but is currently non-functional.
- **Social Media:** Their primary online presence is their official Facebook page, which they use for updates on hours, menus, and hiring.

## Management Structure
- **Owners & Operators:** The cafe is owned and operated directly by **Ben and Judy Bonwick**. 
- **Roles:** Ben Bonwick serves as the Head Chef, while the couple collectively manages the establishment. They have run the cafe for over 25 years.
- There does not appear to be a separate, independent manager; it is a family-run business directly managed by the owners.
